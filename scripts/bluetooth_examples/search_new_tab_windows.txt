Script:

<ctrl+t><delay=100>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ<enter>

Explanation:

This script will create a new tab in your current browser then search for Rick Astley "Never Gonna Give You Up" video.

Steps:
- Creates a new tab
- Waits 100ms for it to open
- Types a URL address
- Hits enter
