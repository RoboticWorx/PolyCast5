Script:

<win+s><delay=500>browser<enter><delay=500>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ<enter>

Explanation:

This script will automatically search for Rick Astley "Never Gonna Give You Up" video.

Steps:
- Opens Windows search
- Waits 500ms for it to open
- Types "browser"
- Selects the browser that comes up
- Waits 500ms for it to open
- Types Rick Astley video URL into the search bar
- Hits enter
